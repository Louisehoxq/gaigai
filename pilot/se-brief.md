# GaiGai pilot: one page for social enterprises (draft)

**What it is.** A free guide that helps people find social enterprises that employ people who are often left out of work, and bookmark places for a weekend outing.

**What we ask of you**
1. Check your listing and tell us what to fix.
2. Put the QR card on your counter.
3. Once a week, note how many customers mentioned GaiGai or showed the card (the tally sheet).
4. A 15-minute chat at the end.

**What we do**
* List you for free, using details you confirm.
* Link to your own website, tagged so you can see GaiGai visits in your analytics.
* Share the anonymous counts for your listing: scans, taps to your website, saves.

**What we don't do**
* Collect donations or payments.
* Store personal data about visitors (no cookies, no accounts).
* Show reviews, ratings or claims about you that you haven't seen.

**What we don't know yet.** Whether this sends enough customers to matter. That's the question the pilot answers, and we will share the result with you either way.
